Practice

Track Time, Seven Days a Week

You need to be a current member of Tokoroa Kart Club Inc to practice on the track. Book a session online and get out there.

Cost

$30 per Session

Each session is $30 and payable at the time of booking.

Track Hours

Daylight Saving8:00am – 8:00pm, Monday to Sunday
Outside Daylight Saving8:00am – 5:00pm, Monday to Sunday

We have no restrictions on who can practice when, including school-age members — we leave that decision to the parent.

Practice day photo coming soon

Gate closing times: The forestry gate at the entrance to the overall airfield/kart track complex is closed at 8:30pm during daylight saving and 5:30pm outside of daylight saving. Please ensure you leave enough time to exit by these times — the club is not responsible should you get locked in.

Booking & Access

How to Book a Practice Session

  • Practice registrations are made from your membership profile in the online portal.
  • Track facility access is provided via a PIN number which is notified to you at the time of booking.
  • When making your practice registration, you only need to select one 10-minute time slot — this gives you access for the entire day.
