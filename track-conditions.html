Track Conditions

Check Before You Head Out

Take a look at our live track webcam to see current conditions at Moffat Road before you make the trip in for practice or a race day.

Live Webcam

Tokoroa Kart Club Track Cam

The feed below updates live from the track. If it looks quiet, grey, or wet — it probably is. Use your own judgement on the day, and remember our practice and race day pages for booking and schedule details.

Remember: this webcam is a helpful guide, not a guarantee. Conditions at ground level (grip, standing water, wind) can differ from what you see on camera — take extra care in changeable South Waikato weather.
