Membership

Ignite Your Passion for Karting

Joining the Tokoroa Kart Club is your gateway to thrilling racing, skill development, and a fantastic community. We welcome karters of all ages and experience levels, from aspiring champions to those simply looking for an exhilarating hobby.

Member Benefits

What You Get as a Member

  • Unlimited track access for practice.
    Enjoy the freedom to hone your skills and get valuable track time on our challenging 765m circuit, available 7 days a week (during daylight hours).
  • Be part of our vibrant community.
    Connect with fellow karting enthusiasts, share knowledge, and build lasting friendships in a supportive, family-friendly environment.
  • Compete in club championships & series.
    Test your mettle and compete for glory in our regular club race meetings and points series.
  • Voting rights & club involvement.
    Have a say in the future of your club by attending meetings and exercising your voting rights on important club matters.
  • Skill development & adrenaline.
    Karting is a fantastic way to improve hand-eye coordination, reflexes, and concentration — all while experiencing the pure exhilaration of speed in a controlled environment.
  • Convenient practice bookings.
    Easily manage your practice sessions through our online Membership Portal, where you'll also receive your track access PIN.
  • Family-friendly sport.
    Karting brings families together, whether you're a driver, mechanic, or cheering on from the sidelines.

Membership Options

Find the Right Membership for You

Racing Member

For individual karters looking to practice and compete.

Family Membership

Ideal for multiple family members residing at the same address, offering access for all.

Social Member

For those who wish to support the club and enjoy the social atmosphere without racing. Social members typically do not have track access for karting.

Bucket Bike & Kayo Bikes

We also welcome the community of bucket bike and Kayo Bike riders to use our facility for practice and racing under the same membership fee structure.
